Editor & Reporter Management Setup

How role permissions and content workflows operate on this platform:

1. Reporter Role (Draft & Submission)

Reporters draft stories and submit them through /admin/reporter. Submissions carry the reporter author level and remain pending review.

2. Editor Role (Review & Direct Publishing)

Editors access /admin/editor to review pending reporter drafts, make revisions, and publish directly to website categories.

3. Super Admin Role (Governance & Security)

Super Admins have unrestricted site access to manage articles across all roles and monitor content streams.
